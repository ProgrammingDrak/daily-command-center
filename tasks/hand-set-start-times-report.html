What is better for you

  • →Set a task to 6 AM, then drag anything else on the day. Your 6 AM stays put. It used to get silently rewritten.
  • →Times in the past are fine. Type any hour you want, including one that already went by.
  • →One early task no longer drags the whole day down with it. Everything else keeps its normal start.
  • →Drag a card to the very top and it can land before a meeting instead of after it.
  • →The amber time marker finally means "I chose this". It used to appear on nearly every row.

Readiness

Ready with caveat

Live on production and verified. The one caveat: existing tasks lose the amber marker on this deploy. Nothing moves and no time changes. The old marker was attached to every timed row, so it could not tell your choices apart from the scheduler's. Re-set a time once and the marker comes back, meaning it for real.

Verified by 2193 automated tests plus a real browser driving the shipped code end to end, including the full round trip through the database.

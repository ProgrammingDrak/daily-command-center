Check-in tasks link back to Slack and to their origin task (PR #327)

Daily Command Center · shipped and live in production

At a glance

  • • A Check in: row told you to chase something and gave you no way to get to it → now one click reaches the Slack message that started it.
  • • Reminders raised off a real task get an Origin task chip that jumps to that task, switching the day for you if it lives elsewhere.
  • • Your existing check-in rows get the link too. Nothing to re-run, no migration.
  • • Opening a task's details now shows its source link for any task from Slack or email, not just check-ins. That panel never had one.
  • • Triage-scheduled tasks stop losing their deeplink to an internal ID that was quietly outranking it.

On a phone

Chips wrap onto their own line. Verified at 390px with no sideways scrolling.

Ready with caveat

Live in production and verified there. One rough edge: taking the origin jump from inside an already-open details panel lands on the right task but with a sparse detail strip. From the row itself it resolves fully.

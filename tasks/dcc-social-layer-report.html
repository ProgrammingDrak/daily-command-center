Take this list with you

Guest, Friend and Coach are one permission model, not three pages. The share link, the reactions, the comments and the reward offers already exist in the repo today; the tier gating, the export bar and the coach role do not.

At a glance

  • Share page grows an export bar and real guest interaction (P1, P2). CSV, calendar file, Markdown for Docs, print-to-PDF, and a date range instead of just today. Comments and reactions get threads on the row plus an owner-side inbox. There is currently zero export code anywhere in the repo, so this lane is greenfield and cheap.
  • Bounty becomes three shapes, and the reward system gets a decision (P3, P4). Today a bounty is only a 2x multiplier, one per day. It grows into: double my points, name a reward, or offer a reward at N points. P4 settles whether slot jackpots stop paying prizes and only pay dollars into the Reserve, with tiered rewards demoted to randomization.
  • The friends layer gets turned on (P5). Friendships, the allowlist, and a completion feed are already written in social-store.js with no UI attached, so nothing in the app can reach them. This is mostly frontend work against finished endpoints.
  • A coach role, in the Monarch advisor mould (P6, biggest lift). Permissions today are tier-only: guest, user, paid. This adds a per-owner grant so one named person gets viewer, commenter, coach, or manager rights: full itinerary, point adjustments, sponsorship approvals, and assigning tasks directly instead of suggesting them.
  • Pets become the social carrier (P7). Leveling and upgrades against the existing decor currency, send your pet to visit a friend's list, and let it carry comments and nudges back. The courier mechanic already exists to build on.

Readiness

READY WITH CAVEAT  The repo audit is done and P1 can start immediately with no schema changes. The caveat is P4: the reward-system question is unanswered, and P3's bounty shapes partly depend on the answer, so P4 should be specced before P3 lands.

Order

  1. 1P1 export, on its own. No schema, no dependencies.
  2. 2P2 and P3 together, with P4 specced as a gate before P3 finishes.
  3. 3P5 friends and feed, which unlocks identity for the next one.
  4. 4P6 coach and manager roles.
  5. 5P7 pets, whenever it sounds fun.
